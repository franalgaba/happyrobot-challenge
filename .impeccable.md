## Design Context

### Users
**Dual audience—same dashboard, two contexts:**

1. **Broker operations** (e.g. Acme Logistics dispatch / sales managers) monitoring day-to-day performance: are calls vetting carriers, matching loads, negotiating within policy, and closing with auditable outcomes?
2. **Vendor evaluation** (HappyRobot prospect meetings, e.g. Carlos Becker) proving product vision and build quality during live demos and follow-ups.

Neither group configures the agent here; both need executive-readable KPIs up top and drill-down tables when asked. Design for confidence in a room, not a power-user control plane.

### Brand Personality
**Minimal, authoritative, operational.**

HappyRobot should feel like a premium enterprise AI product: high contrast, generous whitespace, editorial serif headlines, and crisp sans-serif UI. The voice is confident and factual. It should not feel like a generic admin template, neon “AI” gradients, or consumer chatbot UI.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow HappyRobot marketing: white (`#FFFFFF`) canvas, black (`#000000`) primary UI, medium gray body copy, black pill/rect buttons with white label text, and **sparse** accent color (teal, dusty blue, soft purple) only on small category markers—not full backgrounds.

Typography: elegant **serif** for page and section titles (Instrument Serif); **sans-serif** (Geist) for nav, tables, labels, and metrics. **Geist Mono** for IDs, timestamps, and system codes.

Layout: floating white cards with ~12–16px radius, subtle shadow, optional light dot-grid texture inside panels, thin gray dividers, and soft blurred gradient **behind** hero/summary areas (bokeh), not behind dense data tables.

**Client-oriented framing:** Brand the shell for the broker (“Acme Logistics”) with HappyRobot as the platform vendor in the footer or secondary line—this is their operations view, not HappyRobot’s internal console.

**Light and dark themes** with official logo variants (`happyrobot-logo-light.png` / `happyrobot-logo-dark.png`) and a header theme switcher (preference stored in `localStorage`). No client-specified anti-references beyond the defaults above (avoid dark hacker UI, rainbow gradients, cluttered sidebars, chartjunk).

**Accessibility:** No extra WCAG or accommodation requirements beyond sensible defaults (contrast, touch targets, `prefers-reduced-motion`).

### Design Principles
1. **Metrics answer business questions first**—booked vs rejected, negotiation success, verification source, sentiment—before technical IDs.
2. **Legibility over decoration**—tables and KPIs stay clean; gradient and grid are ambient, not competing with numbers.
3. **Serif signals narrative; sans signals action**—headlines and section titles use serif; controls and data use sans.
4. **Accent is categorical, not decorative**—color marks outcome/sentiment/status, not entire panels.
5. **Respect reduced motion**—no essential information in animation; prefer static layout with optional gentle refresh.
